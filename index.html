AI-powered Windows video translation workflow for Arabic subtitles.

Dubora automates video translation through audio extraction, speech transcription, contextual Arabic translation, subtitle validation, and final video rendering while keeping subtitle timing under application control.

  • AI speech transcription and contextual Arabic translation
  • Preserved subtitle timestamps
  • FFmpeg-based audio extraction and rendering
